Project Highlights

Matrix-Calculator SoC

Repo

Purdue System on Chips Team · SystemVerilog, Yosys, Virtuoso, SimVision

A 33-state FSM in SystemVerilog driving a hardware calculator for 9×9 matrix operations, verified with 7 module-level testbenches and run on an FPGA simulator. The design uses 3×128-entry memory and a 2-signal handshake bus connecting the controller to 4 arithmetic modules, with Yosys synthesis constraints resolved through shift-based address offsets. Carried through a full RTL-to-GDS flow: logic synthesis, place and route, and physical timing closure, with standard-cell layouts in Virtuoso verified in SimVision.

Error-Correction Coding with Transformers

Repo

NYCU Institute of Communications · PyTorch, CUDA, Linux

Transformer encoder-decoder architectures for error-correction coding, trained on a 4.6 GB HDF5 edit-distance dataset with CUDA GPU acceleration. I characterized synchronization-error channels across 1M binary codewords and built Levenshtein alignment metrics to compare autoregressive, embedding, and CTC decoder architectures, benchmarked against BCJR and CNN-ED baselines on BER, BLER, and Hamming loss.

Humanoid Walking Analysis

Repo

Purdue TRACE Humanoid Robotics Lab · MATLAB, AnyBody, Pinocchio

Spatial kinematics and inverse dynamics simulated in AnyBody from 3D ViCon motion-capture data, processed in MATLAB. I consolidated a 16-module extraction, plotting, and reporting workflow into a single pipeline handling inverse-dynamics and marker-tracking HDF5 trees exceeding 30,000 nodes, validated AnyBody-predicted ground reaction force against measured data across 10+ joint force and moment channels, and cross-checked joint torque and force in Pinocchio.
