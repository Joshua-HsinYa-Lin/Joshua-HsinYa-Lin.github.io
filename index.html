A resume says what I built. These projects let you operate the ideas behind it: spin a reaction wheel, step through an FPGA state machine, corrupt a codeword, overload a battery pack, or hear an equalizer.

Every page labels what you are looking at. Measured is bench or lab data. Simulation is physics computed on the page from the real design. Illustrative uses typical or generic values chosen so the behavior is visible, never presented as something I measured.

Projects

  • A matrix coprocessor on an FPGA

    Type a matrix on the keypad and watch a 33-state machine, three memory regions and a valid/ready handshake do the arithmetic, one clock at a time. Purdue SoCET · Jan 2026 to May 2026

  • A Kalman filter in hardware in progress

    A RISC-V system on chip with an attitude filter accelerator. Watch a Kalman filter fuse a gyro and an accelerometer, then see how the whole filter becomes a straight line of descriptors over one multiplier. Purdue SoCET · Fall 2026

  • Pointing a satellite with spinning wheels

    Spin a wheel one way and the body turns the other. Slew a satellite, split torque across a four-wheel pyramid, and feel what 727 ms of latency does to a control loop. Taiwan Space Agency · Jul to Aug 2026

  • A boost converter and an equalizer, measured against their simulations

    Drag the duty cycle and watch the inductor pump 5 V up to 30 V. Move three faders and hear the bands. Then see why the bench read 9.5% below the simulation. Purdue ECE 20007 · Spring 2026

  • The board that disconnects the battery

    Six cells, two stacked protection ICs, and a load. Overload it, overcharge it, overheat it, and watch the FETs open before anything else does. Purdue Lunabotics · Sep 2025 to Aug 2026

  • Decoding bits that moved

    Delete one bit and every bit after it reads as wrong. Corrupt a codeword, compare position scoring against alignment, and see why a Transformer was pointed at it. NYCU Institute of Communications · May to Jul 2026

  • Wiring a robot so a fault finds itself

    A short appears somewhere in the harness. Find it in a single harness, then in one with a labeled connector at every subsystem. Count your steps. FIRST Robotics Team 8020 · Aug 2023 to Jun 2024

  • How much of your weight does a helper carry?

    Lean on a rod held by someone else and watch the knee unload, the ankle push sideways, and the helper's wrist take the difference. Then compare against motion capture. Purdue TRACE Laboratory · Jan 2026 to present
