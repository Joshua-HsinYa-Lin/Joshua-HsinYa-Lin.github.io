Adjustable DC-DC Boost Converter

Purdue ECE 20007 · LTspice, oscilloscope, discrete power stage

An adjustable boost converter stepping a 5V input up to a 5V–30V output range, with the switching duty cycle sized from the boost transfer function rather than tuned empirically. A 555 timer generates the PWM driving an NMOS switch into an inductor. A PMOS output ceiling limiter holds the rail below its maximum under no load or a shorted load, a failure mode that otherwise destroys the output capacitors. Measured output reconciled against LTspice by accounting for wire and breadboard resistance, component tolerance, and device on-resistance.

Three-Band Analog Audio Equalizer

Purdue ECE 20007 · Analog design, op-amps, LTspice

A three-band analog equalizer splitting an input signal into low, mid, and high bands with active RC filters, each band independently adjustable. LM324 op-amps act as unity-gain buffers between the passive filters and the volume stages to remove loading effects, with per-band inverting amplifiers setting gain. The bands recombine without cross-loading and drive a speaker through a power amplifier stage delivering over 400 mW into 8 Ω. Simulated with an AC sweep in LTspice before building.
