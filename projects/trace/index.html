How much of your weight does a helper carry?

Jan 2026 to present · West Lafayette, Indiana

Before a humanoid robot can help someone walk, you need to know what forces a human helper actually provides. I take motion capture of two people walking together, drive a musculoskeletal simulation with it, and pull the joint forces out.

The interactive version of this page is being built. What is here now is the summary and the figures.

What I did

  • Processed 3D ViCon motion-capture data in MATLAB to drive AnyBody simulations of spatial kinematics and inverse dynamics for human-robot interaction systems.
  • Extracted and analyzed per-joint forces and torques, comparing the assisted side against the unassisted side to quantify how much load a helper actually removes.
  • Tested whether AnyBody's residual Hand of God force was masking failed ground reaction force prediction, reporting R² 0.03 and cosine similarity 0.58 as evidence against the hypothesis across 10+ force and moment channels.
  • Consolidated a 16-module extraction, plotting and reporting workflow into a single pipeline with fallback path resolution that survives AnyBody changing its output layout.
